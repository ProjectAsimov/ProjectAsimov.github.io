Delete your Tessera account

Applies to accounts created by signing in with Google

In the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This deletes your tasks, days, tiles and freezes, the groups you host, and your membership in other groups, on every device, immediately. It cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app

Email tasktracker.support@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Tessera account". We delete the account and all server data within 30 days and confirm by reply. We do not keep a copy afterwards.

Deleting only part of your data

Delete a single task in the app to remove it and its days. Leave a group to remove your membership and your name from that group's leaderboard. See the privacy policy for what is stored.
