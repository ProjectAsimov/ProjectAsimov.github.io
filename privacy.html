TaskTracker privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

TaskTracker is a small app for tracking yes/no daily tasks. This page explains what it stores and why. The short version: it keeps the tasks and days you enter, and, if you sign in, your Google account's basic profile so your data can follow you between devices. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than running the app.

What is stored

  • Your tasks and days. Task names, colours, icons, and which days you marked. Without signing in this stays only on your device.
  • Your Google account basics, if you sign in. Your Google account ID, display name, and email address, received from Google when you sign in. Used to tie your data to you and to show your name to members of any group you join.
  • Group membership. If you share a task or join one, the app stores which groups you belong to so members can see a leaderboard of each other's streaks and counts.

Where it is stored

On your device, in the browser's storage. When you sign in, a copy is stored on servers run by Cloudflare (Workers, D1, and KV) so it can sync to your other devices. Sign-in itself is handled by Google; TaskTracker never sees your password.

What is not collected

No advertising identifiers, no analytics or tracking, no location, no contacts, and nothing beyond what is listed above.

Who can see your data

Only you, and, for shared tasks, the other members of that group (your name and your streak, month, and total counts for that task). Nobody else, and no third parties beyond the hosting providers named above.

Deleting your data

Delete a task in the app to remove it and its days. Sign out to remove the link between the device and your account. To delete your account and everything stored on the server, email tasktracker.support@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and it will be removed within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Questions: tasktracker.support@gmail.com.
