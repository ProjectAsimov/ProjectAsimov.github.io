Tessera community guidelines

For shared tasks and groups

Groups are small and private: only people with the invite link can join, and members see each other's display name, streak, counts for the shared task, and shoutouts. Keep it worth being in.

The rules

  • Use a name you would use in person. Your display name comes from your Google account; no slurs, impersonation or contact details in it.
  • Shoutouts are encouragement. One per member per day; there is no messaging, by design.
  • Mark your own days honestly. Freezes and repairs are shown to the group as such.
  • Hosts can remove members; anyone can leave. No one is kept in a group.

Report and block

From a task's Members list you can block a member, which hides them from your leaderboard and stops shoutouts between you, or report them with a short reason. Reports go to us; we read every one and may remove a member, close a group, or delete an account. You can also write to tasktracker.support@gmail.com.

Content we remove

Harassment, hate, sexual content, threats, spam, and anything illegal. Repeated reports lead to account deletion.
